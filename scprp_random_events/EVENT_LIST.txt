SCP-RP RANDOM EVENTS
Build: 2.5.2-610-biomass-model

Start an event with:
scprp_event_start <event_id>


1. CONTAINMENT CALIBRATION

Command:
scprp_event_start containment_calibration

Calibration stations appear around the site. Research holds E to calibrate each
station, then Security presses E to authorize it. Completing every station grants
Foundation personnel additional armor.


2. SCP-999 MORALE TOUR

Command:
scprp_event_start scp999_morale_tour

SCP-999 begins a supervised facility tour. Nearby non-SCP personnel periodically
receive health, armor, and a small movement-speed boost.


3. COGNITOHAZARD INTERVIEW

Command:
scprp_event_start cognitohazard_interview

A player becomes exposed to a cognitohazard and receives strange messages and
sounds. Exposure can spread to nearby personnel. Research must locate exposed
players and press E on them to complete stabilization interviews.


4. SCP OBJECT TESTING REQUEST

Command:
scprp_event_start object_testing_request

An anomalous object appears near active personnel. Research must bring a test
subject close and hold E on the object. Each test produces a random effect,
including healing, damage, hallucinations, movement changes, armor, or physical
disturbances.


5. TEMPORAL RESEARCH INCIDENT

Command:
scprp_event_start temporal_research_incident

A temporal rift and several anchor devices appear. Research must hold E on every
anchor. Personnel near the rift may be slowed, accelerated, launched, hear strange
sounds, or receive messages from another time.


6. D-CLASS MEDICAL SCREENING

Command:
scprp_event_start dclass_medical_screening

Selected D-Class are marked for emergency screening. Medical or Research personnel
press E on each subject. One subject may be an anomalous carrier who causes
coughing, slowdown, and mental static until stabilized. Cooperative D-Class receive
a small reward.


7. REDACTED OBJECT MANIFEST

Command:
scprp_event_start redacted_object_manifest

Several unidentified anomalous objects appear around the site. Research must locate
and classify them by holding E. Objects can be harmless, unstable, biological,
energetic, cognitohazardous, or spatially distorted.


8. REALITY SINKHOLE

Command:
scprp_event_start reality_sinkhole

A reality-warping zone forms inside the facility with anchors around it. Research
must stabilize every anchor. Players near the sinkhole may be pushed away, slowed,
launched, or receive strange reality-distortion messages.


9. POWER GRID SURGE

Command:
scprp_event_start power_grid_surge

Site 70's actual facility lights flicker and shut down. Emergency power terminals
appear around the map and must be stabilized. Completing every terminal restores
power. Stopping or timing out the event also automatically restores the lights.


10. SCP-008 OUTBREAK

Command:
scprp_event_start scp008_outbreak

SCP-008 canisters rupture around the site and infected NPCs appear. Nearby personnel
build exposure and become infectious at 60 percent. At 100 percent, they transform
into an SCP-008-2 instance with a zombie model and SWEP.

Medical or Research personnel can administer suppressants, while responders
neutralize the leaking canisters.


11. SCP-610 BIOMASS BLOOM

Command:
scprp_event_start scp610_biomass_bloom

Destructible SCP-610 flesh growths appear and periodically produce hostile infected
NPCs. Standing near biomass builds exposure. At 100 percent, the player transforms
into an SCP-610 instance.

Armed personnel must shoot and destroy all biomass growths before time expires.


OTHER ADMIN COMMANDS

scprp_event_list
Displays all registered event IDs.

scprp_event_debug
Displays the loaded addon build and event status.

scprp_event_roles
Displays living role counts detected by the addon.

scprp_event_stop
Stops the active event.

scprp_event_next
Triggers the warning for the next random event.
